<div class="mb-12">
  <h2 class="text-xl font-bold mb-4 section-title section-title--mb-6">Context</h2>
  <p>
    The original franchise site &mdash; built for the Kyiv office &mdash; went down during the full-scale invasion. The Lviv franchise needed a new digital presence urgently, but more importantly, they needed one that actually worked for their local market.
  </p>
  <blockquote class="border-l-[3px] border-accent-500 pl-6 py-3 my-6">
    <p class="text-lg font-medium text-gray-700 leading-relaxed m-0">Research revealed the core issue wasn't just missing functionality &mdash; it was <strong>trust</strong>.</p>
  </blockquote>
  <p>
    Most local leads came through Instagram, but Instagram alone couldn't close serious clients. There was also a new audience: many displaced families who had fled to Lviv from other regions were searching for nannies directly on <strong>Google</strong> &mdash; they had no local Instagram network to discover CoffeeNanny through. Without a website, this entire segment was completely unreachable.
  </p>
  <p>
    Families needed to see pricing, understand how the service worked, and feel confident before trusting someone with their child. A proper website wasn't just nice to have &mdash; it was the only channel that could serve both local and displaced families effectively.
  </p>
</div>

<div class="mb-12">
  <h2 class="text-xl font-bold mb-4 section-title section-title--mb-6">Approach</h2>
  <div class="approach-timeline">

<div class="approach-step">
  <span class="approach-step__num">01 &middot; Discover</span>
  <h3>UX Audit &amp; Competitive Research</h3>
  <p>I started with a UX audit of the previous franchise-wide site. Key finding: it felt generic &mdash; no local presence, no Lviv-specific content, no clear trust signals for a childcare service. I also researched direct and indirect competitors to identify what they did well and where CoffeeNanny could differentiate. This analysis shaped our positioning and content strategy from day one.</p>
</div>

<div class="approach-step">
  <span class="approach-step__num">02 &middot; Listen</span>
  <h3>Client &amp; Stakeholder Interviews</h3>
  <p>I interviewed existing clients to understand what drove their trust, how they discovered the service, and what nearly stopped them from booking. Conversations with the franchise owner clarified business priorities and the roadmap.</p>
</div>

<div class="approach-step">
  <span class="approach-step__num">03 &middot; Design</span>
  <h3>Figma + AI-Assisted Style Exploration</h3>
  <p>Based on research insights, I designed the full site in Figma &mdash; information architecture, wireframes, and high-fidelity UI. To speed things up, I used one of the first AI design tools available in 2022 to generate style variants and explore different visual directions rapidly. This let us converge on the right look much faster than iterating manually.</p>
</div>

<div class="approach-step">
  <span class="approach-step__num">04 &middot; Ship</span>
  <h3>MVP on Wix</h3>
  <p>To ship fast, I built the MVP directly on Wix &mdash; leveraging its CMS, SEO tools, and hosting to get the site live in days rather than weeks. This allowed the franchise to immediately start generating inquiries.</p>
</div>

<div class="approach-step">
  <span class="approach-step__num">05 &middot; Iterate</span>
  <h3>Data-Informed Iteration</h3>
  <p>After launch, I analysed user behaviour and conversion data to refine the experience &mdash; A/B testing messaging, adjusting the booking flow, and improving content based on what actually drove inquiries.</p>
</div>

</div>
</div>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold mb-2">Traffic Growth (May 2022 &rarr; 2026)</h3>
  <p>
    The site launched with almost no traffic and a <strong>90% bounce rate</strong>. Within months it was attracting thousands of visitors per month &mdash; a <strong>20&times; increase</strong> in traffic driven entirely by organic search and social media, with no paid advertising.
  </p>

  <h3 class="text-base font-semibold mb-2">Engagement Quality</h3>
  <p>
    The bounce rate dropped from <strong>90% at launch to ~55%</strong> &mdash; a clear signal that the UX and content structure worked: visitors were finding what they needed instead of leaving immediately. Average session duration grew to <strong>2&ndash;4 minutes</strong>, indicating serious engagement from potential clients.
  </p>
