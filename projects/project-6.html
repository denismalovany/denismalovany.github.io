TactiField Military Interface

I designed a tactical display system for field operations that prioritizes critical information under pressure, using clear visual hierarchy, consistent symbology, and minimal cognitive load to support rapid decision-making in high-stakes environments.

This interface applies principles of perceptible affordances and forgiving design to ensure that operators can quickly understand the situation and take appropriate action, even when fatigued or operating in challenging environmental conditions.
