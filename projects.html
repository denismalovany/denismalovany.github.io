CoffeeNanny Lviv Franchise Website

CoffeeNanny helps families find trusted nannies for their children. But without a proper website, the Lviv franchise had a trust problem — serious clients needed transparency, pricing, and a professional channel before trusting their kids to a new service. Especially during wartime, many displaced families were searching for nannies on Google, not local Instagram pages — a website was the only way to reach them. I researched direct and indirect competitors to identify key differentiators, then led UX audit, client interviews, Figma design, and a Wix MVP that turned the business from near-collapse to stable growth. Today ~10% of new clients come directly through the website, with even more social leads crediting the site for their trust.

Research-driven design and rapid MVP development solved a critical trust gap — turning a struggling franchise into a stable, growing business.

NovaCheckout Redesign

I led the redesign of an e-commerce checkout flow focused on reducing friction and building trust through clear progress indicators, transparent pricing, and streamlined form interactions that respect users' time and intention to purchase.

By simplifying complex form fields and providing real-time validation, this redesign aims to increase conversion rates while maintaining the security and reliability users expect from online transactions.

InsightView Analytics Dashboard

I created a data visualization tool that transforms complex datasets into clear, actionable insights through thoughtful information hierarchy, contextual help, and customizable views that adapt to different user expertise levels.

This dashboard balances depth and accessibility, allowing both novice users to grasp key trends and expert analysts to drill down into specific metrics without feeling overwhelmed or under-supported.

Nexus Smart Home Controller

I designed an IoT interface for managing connected home devices that prioritizes simplicity and consistency, allowing users to control lighting, climate, and security systems through familiar patterns that reduce the learning curve for smart home technology.

The design focuses on creating mental models that transfer across different device types, so users can confidently control new devices without relearning entire interaction patterns for each product in their ecosystem.

ResearchLens Data Visualization Tool

I developed a specialized visualization platform for scientific researchers that transforms complex experimental data into clear visual narratives, helping scientists identify patterns, validate hypotheses, and communicate findings more effectively.

By combining rigorous data accuracy with thoughtful visual storytelling, this tool bridges the gap between complex analysis and accessible interpretation, supporting both detailed exploration and clear presentation of research findings.

TactiField Military Interface

I designed a tactical display system for field operations that prioritizes critical information under pressure, using clear visual hierarchy, consistent symbology, and minimal cognitive load to support rapid decision-making in high-stakes environments.

This interface applies principles of perceptible affordances and forgiving design to ensure that operators can quickly understand the situation and take appropriate action, even when fatigued or operating in challenging environmental conditions.
