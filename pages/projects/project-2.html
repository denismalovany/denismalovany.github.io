Case Study

NovaCheckout Redesign

I led the redesign of an e-commerce checkout flow focused on reducing friction and building trust through clear progress indicators, transparent pricing, and streamlined form interactions that respect users' time and intention to purchase.

By simplifying complex form fields and providing real-time validation, this redesign aims to increase conversion rates while maintaining the security and reliability users expect from online transactions.

Context

Cart abandonment is one of the most persistent challenges in e-commerce, with industry averages hovering around 70%. The NovaCheckout platform had a checkout flow that was functional but friction-heavy — field labels were inconsistent, error states were confusing, and the lack of a progress indicator left users uncertain about how many steps remained.

Through heuristic evaluation and session replay analysis, I identified three primary pain points: unclear pricing breakdowns that eroded trust, form interactions that felt punitive rather than helpful, and a mobile experience that added significant cognitive overhead. The goal was to transform the checkout from a necessary hurdle into a seamless, confidence-building conclusion to the shopping journey.

Approach

1. UX Audit & Heuristic Evaluation

I conducted a thorough heuristic evaluation against Nielsen's usability principles, identifying 23 friction points across the checkout flow. Key findings included inconsistent error messaging, poor mobile tap-target sizing, and ambiguous field grouping that confused users about which information belonged together.

2. User Flow Mapping & Task Analysis

I mapped the complete checkout journey — from cart to confirmation — identifying where users dropped off and which steps caused the most hesitation. Session replays and analytics data revealed that the shipping address form alone accounted for 40% of all abandoned checkouts.

3. Wireframing & Prototype Design

I designed revised flows in Figma, introducing a persistent progress indicator, contextual error messages with inline validation, and a transparent pricing summary that updated in real-time as users made selections. The prototype was built for both desktop and mobile breakpoints.

4. Usability Testing with Real Users

Moderated usability tests with 8 participants validated the redesign against the original flow. Task completion rate improved by 25%, and users consistently praised the progress indicator and real-time validation as confidence-building features.

5. Design Handoff & Developer Collaboration

I created a comprehensive design spec with component breakdowns, interaction states, and accessibility annotations. Worked closely with the engineering team through implementation to ensure the live experience matched the designed intent.

Result

The redesigned checkout flow demonstrated meaningful improvements in both quantitative and qualitative measures. Task completion rate in usability tests improved by 25%, with participants reporting significantly higher confidence in the purchase process.

Early analytics projected a meaningful uplift in conversion rates and a reduction in customer support tickets related to checkout errors. The real-time validation and transparent pricing summary were cited as the most impactful features by test participants.

The project demonstrated that checkout UX — often treated as an afterthought — can be transformed into a competitive advantage through methodical research, thoughtful interaction design, and close developer collaboration.
