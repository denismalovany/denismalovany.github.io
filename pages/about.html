About Me

I'm Denys Malovanyi, a product designer and frontend developer with over 9 years of experience spanning UI/UX design, frontend development, and mechanical engineering. I currently work as a Mechanical Engineer and Product Designer at VOL Volunteer Optical Laboratory, creating user-centered digital and physical products.

Beyond traditional design and development skills, I experiment with AI agents and local LLM deployment to enhance my design research, prototyping, and workflow automation—bringing emerging technology principles into practical design solutions.

My career began in frontend development at BITECC GmbH and InvestGo24 in 2015, where I built responsive interfaces and landing pages. I then grew into full-stack UI roles at Codemotion Ninjas and Cygnati Group, working on diverse projects from e-commerce platforms to medical information portals and mobile applications.

At HebronSoft, I transitioned fully into UI/UX design, leading design projects and mentoring junior designers. Following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, I dedicated myself to full-time volunteer work supporting the Armed Forces of Ukraine — an experience that deepened my skills in crisis management, logistics, and operational planning under pressure.

My approach combines rigorous research with thoughtful aesthetics. I believe great design emerges from understanding user needs deeply, translating insights into intuitive interfaces, and refining every detail through iterative prototyping and testing.

I'm proficient in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and modern design tools. I thrive in cross-functional teams and enjoy bridging the gap between design and development to deliver polished, accessible, and performant products.
